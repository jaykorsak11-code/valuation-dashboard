Model Methodology & Formulas Culverhouse Finance Dashboard
1. Revenue & Income Path:

$\text{Rev}_N = \text{Rev}_0 \times (1 + g)^N$
$\text{Net Income}_N = \text{Rev}_N \times \text{Margin}$

2. Earnings & Share Target:

$\text{EPS}_N = \frac{\text{Net Income}_N}{\text{Diluted Shares}}$
$\text{Target Price} = \text{EPS}_N \times \text{Exit P/E}$

3. Returns & Annualized CAGR:

$\text{Upside} = \frac{\text{Target}}{\text{Price}_0} - 1$
$\text{CAGR} = \left(\frac{\text{Target}}{\text{Price}_0}\right)^{1/N} - 1$
